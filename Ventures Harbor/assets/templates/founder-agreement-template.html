Founder Agreement

This Founder Agreement ("Agreement") is made on [Date] among the following founders of [Asset / Company Name] (the "Asset"):

Founder NameRoleEquity %Initial Contribution
[Name][e.g. Active Partner / Operations][__%]₹[Amount]
[Name][e.g. Silent Partner / Investor][__%]₹[Amount]

1. Purpose

The founders intend to jointly build, operate, and grow the Asset described as: [brief description of the business].

2. Roles and Responsibilities

Each founder's role and expected time commitment is as set out in the table above. Active partners are expected to commit at least [X hours/week] to the Asset's operations. Silent partners are not required to participate in day-to-day operations.

3. Vesting Schedule

Equity/profit-share allocated to each founder shall vest over [e.g. 3 years], with a [e.g. 1-year] cliff, meaning no equity vests until the founder has been involved for at least that period. After the cliff, the remaining equity vests [monthly/quarterly] in equal installments.

4. Intellectual Property Assignment

All intellectual property created by a founder in connection with the Asset — including but not limited to business plans, branding, code, designs, and processes — shall be assigned to the Asset upon creation.

5. Decision-Making

Day-to-day decisions may be made by the active/operating founder(s). Major decisions (e.g. raising capital, taking on debt above ₹[Amount], admitting a new partner, or dissolving the Asset) require agreement of founders holding at least [e.g. 75%] of total equity/profit-share.

6. Founder Exit / Leaver Provisions

If a founder leaves before their equity is fully vested:

  • Good Leaver (e.g. health reasons, mutual agreement): retains all vested equity as of the exit date.
  • Bad Leaver (e.g. breach of this agreement, fraud, competing Asset): the Asset/remaining founders may buy back vested equity at a pre-agreed valuation or nominal value, as decided by the remaining founders.

7. Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation

While active in the Asset, and for [e.g. 12 months] after exit, a founder shall not start or join a directly competing business, nor solicit the Asset's employees, partners, or customers, within [geographic scope, if applicable].

8. Formalization

The founders agree that this Agreement is a precursor to formal incorporation (e.g. as an LLP or Private Limited Company) and that a formal partnership deed / shareholders' agreement will be executed to reflect these terms once the entity is registered.

9. Dispute Resolution

Disputes shall first be discussed in good faith among the founders. If unresolved within [e.g. 30 days], the matter shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in [City].

10. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
