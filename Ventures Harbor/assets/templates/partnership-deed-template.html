Deed of Partnership

This Deed of Partnership is made on this [DATE] day of [MONTH, YEAR] between:

Party 1: [Full Name], son/daughter of [Father's Name], residing at [Address] (hereinafter referred to as "Partner 1"),

Party 2: [Full Name], son/daughter of [Father's Name], residing at [Address] (hereinafter referred to as "Partner 2"),

(collectively referred to as "the Partners")

1. Name and Nature of Business

The Partners have agreed to carry on the business of [nature of business, e.g. "car detailing services"] under the name and style of [Asset / Firm Name] (hereinafter "the Firm").

2. Principal Place of Business

The principal place of business of the Firm shall be at [Address], or such other place(s) as the Partners may mutually decide.

3. Duration

The partnership shall commence from [Date] and shall continue until dissolved in accordance with this deed or by mutual consent.

4. Capital Contribution

  1. Partner 1 shall contribute capital of ₹[Amount].
  2. Partner 2 shall contribute capital of ₹[Amount].
  3. Additional capital, if required, shall be contributed by the Partners in the same proportion unless otherwise agreed in writing.

5. Profit and Loss Sharing Ratio

The net profits and losses of the Firm shall be shared between the Partners in the following ratio:

  • Partner 1: [__%]
  • Partner 2: [__%]

6. Roles and Responsibilities

The day-to-day management and operations of the Firm shall be handled by [Active Partner name(s)]. Silent partners shall not participate in day-to-day operations but retain the rights specified in this deed.

7. Bank Account and Financial Records

The Firm shall maintain a bank account in its name, operated jointly or as authorized by the Partners. Proper books of accounts shall be maintained and made available for inspection by any Partner at all reasonable times.

8. Admission of a New Partner

No new partner shall be admitted into the Firm without the written consent of all existing Partners.

9. Retirement / Exit of a Partner

A Partner intending to retire from the Firm shall give at least [30/60/90] days' written notice to the other Partners. The retiring Partner's capital and share of profits up to the date of retirement shall be settled within [__] days of retirement.

10. Dissolution

The partnership may be dissolved by mutual written consent of all Partners, or as per the provisions of the Indian Partnership Act, 1932. On dissolution, the assets and liabilities of the Firm shall be settled and distributed among the Partners as per their capital and profit-sharing ratio, after payment of all outstanding liabilities.

11. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising out of this deed shall first be resolved through mutual discussion. If unresolved, disputes shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with the venue of arbitration at [City].

12. Governing Law

This deed shall be governed by the laws of India, including the Indian Partnership Act, 1932.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Partners have set their hands on the day, month, and year first above written.
